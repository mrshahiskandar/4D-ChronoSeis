4D ChronoSeis app (APGCE 2026 GeoHackathon, Challenge 3).
The app runs the team's notebooks from https://github.com/dankkl89/Shell_Geohackathon unchanged (see README.md):
clone both repos on the HPC and point the app at the notebook folder.
No seismic, well or output data is included. Start on the HPC with: cd 4D-ChronoSeis && ./start_app.sh
